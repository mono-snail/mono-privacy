MonoProxy Data Deletion

Last updated: August 13, 2026

MonoProxy does not use user accounts. Proxy traffic, credentials, browsing history, device policies, and traffic statistics remain on your device and are not uploaded to Monoware.

Delete data stored on your device

  1. Use the clear or reset controls in MonoProxy to remove optional history, traffic totals, and settings.
  2. Uninstall MonoProxy to remove the app's remaining local data.

Local app data is removed immediately when you clear it or uninstall the app. Monoware does not have a server copy of this data.

Delete a support submission

If you voluntarily submitted feedback through MonoProxy, you may request deletion of the submitted email address, title, description, app and device details, and limited proxy configuration summary.

  1. Email support@monoware.app with the subject MonoProxy data deletion request.
  2. Include the feedback ID, the email address used in the submission, and the approximate submission date so we can locate it.
  3. We will verify the request and delete the matching support submission, normally within 30 days.

We may retain only the minimum information required to comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, or document completion of the deletion request. Any such information is kept only for the period required for that purpose.
